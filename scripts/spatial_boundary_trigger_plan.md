# Spatial Boundary & Transfer-Trigger System + Station/Area Realm — vetted plan

## SUMMARY
We build the MISSING geometric transfer TRIGGER (the autonomous producer that replaces today's #[cfg(test)] transient seeders) plus the Station/Area realm arms, entirely as append-only additions to already-proven machinery: one vd-core Boundary primitive (Shell/Aabb/Obb over the existing swept-crossing + hysteresis-band), one per-shard bevy system evaluate_realm_boundaries wired into the verbatim 9-system schedule, and ONE dest-resolution path. It is robust because every crossing decision is Category-A integer-gated (LatticePos.cell in/out + n_entry + k_dwell counters; f64 only refines sub-cell) with the swept segment (never a point) as the anti-tunnel core; scalable because Slices 5/6 (uniform-cell index shared with D-9 AoI, then pure-map/ordered-reduce rayon) are strictly additive over an O(n·m) correctness core; DRY because a Station/Area flows through the ONE saga/TransientBatch/Fence machinery the instant the enum arm exists (no feature ever matches a realm kind — HR3), Obb is a shim over Aabb, and dock/undock/three-deep-exit are all ordinary single-level transfers differing only in the (from_realm,to_realm) tuple; non-cornering because signals ride the Interest substrate, block-built stations are ShardProfile DATA (profiles::station), and the parent-chain is single-truth for both trigger and multi-mesh render. The adversary's two correctness holes (signed-distance vs radial-distance units collision; box-corner hysteresis) are folded in as the FIRST gates of Slice 1, and its highest-leverage structural fix — route BOTH transient and durable crossings through the orchestrator's DirectoryCore::head(Realm) instead of an unbuilt shard-local neighbor cache — is adopted, dissolving the chicken-and-egg dest problem and unifying both fan-out arms onto one dest-authority (HR3).
