## Overall verdict

**REFUTED.** The mathematics at the heart of the slice survives the attack — `W(1) = 1` is guaranteed
by the compile-time assertion rather than sampled, the four Newton steps converge from `a₀ = t` with a
derivative bounded in `[0.785, 1.558]`, no iterate ever leaves the face, the worst residual is
7.45 × 10⁻⁹ cells at the largest legal body against a gate of 10⁻⁶, the four seam records I derived by
