plan `slice_03_registry_catalogue.md`, the design sources, and the laws HR3, HR5, SL5 and SL10.

Read-only. I ran no build, no test and no coverage run. Every number below is a hand computation from
the source, and I say so where a claim is a prediction and not a measurement.

## Overall verdict

The slice is REFUTED on three findings, and every one of them is load-bearing for a world that is
saved. **First (F1), the kind numbering is not append-only.** The recipe builds Empty, then the terrain
substances, then the building substances times twenty shapes, so appending one terrain substance —
which is precisely what slice 5's geology and the owner's "way more substances" require — shifts every
building kind and both holder rows. A saved oak cube becomes a chalk hillside, and no test in the tree
sees the shift; the module doc says a pinned digest makes a reorder loud, and no such pin exists (F8).
**Second (F2), the integrity derivation returns zero for dirt, loam, sand, gravel, silt, mud, peat,
snow, ice and ash**, because the joules-to-kilojoules divide floors before the square root; the design's
own worked table says packed soil is 169 points and water ice is 207, and the test hides the same error
on steel, glass and concrete behind tolerance ranges (F24). I hand-checked a fix — take the root in
joules at 1 024ths — that reproduces all nine of the design's published numbers exactly. **Third (F3
with F4), the identity digest does not bind what a saved record actually means.** It folds the
substance NUMBER and never the substance's identity, so editing row 2 from granite to chalk opens every
store in the galaxy silently, while renaming the "hud" attachment refuses every store; and it folds the
gap byte's step count but neither its SIGN nor its sample point, which the plan and domain 04 both put
inside the digest and call a one-way door. Below those, the scale mask promises a 1/16 m block the
twelve-byte record cannot carry (F5), no rotation rule exists at all although the plan's decode gate
names one (F6), `Rotation::code` hands a reflection a real orientation code and a test blesses it (F7),
and the 200-microsecond measurement has a harness that asserts nothing and a number nobody has written
down (F9). What genuinely stands is worth saying: the twenty shape rows match the base's catalogue row
for row, the face bit numbering matches the grid, the rotation table is the true group and is pinned,
and the prefix property is correct for the two tables the digest does cover.
