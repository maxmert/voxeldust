## What a reader should do first

1. **Read F1 and F2.** The corner phantom does not work. No chunk owns an edge that touches it, so
   the mesh has a hole at all eight cube corners of every body at every rung. Worse, the three faces
   that meet at a corner see three DIFFERENT four-cycles of the same four columns, so they cannot
   agree on the corner vertex either. `lattice.rs`'s module doc states the opposite as a fact.
2. **Read F3.** Across a seam the two chunks' local axes run opposite ways. Round-half-up is not
   symmetric under that flip, so a seam vertex can land one quantum apart on the two sides. The test
   that is supposed to catch this asserts `twins > 0`.
3. **Read F5 and F6.** The byte-for-byte gate misses the partial chunk — the newest code path — and
   the login identity does not fold the mesh at all, so a client whose extractor drifts is admitted.
4. Then F9 (a neighbour's edit cannot reach this chunk's halo, so the first player who digs on a
   chunk edge makes a crack) and F11 (M6-2's 4 ms gate is not met and was replaced by a 300 ms one).
