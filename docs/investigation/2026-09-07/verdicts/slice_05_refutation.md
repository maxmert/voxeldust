# Slice 5 — THE GENERATOR: the refutation

Read-only attack on the landed slice (uncommitted tree), against CLAUDE.md's laws, ruling V9 and
`docs/investigation/2026-09-07/slice_05_generator.md` §1–§11. Nothing was built, run or measured: the
machine is owned by another job, so every row is a READ of the source, and a row that needed a run is
marked UNMEASURED and says so.

**Counts: 30 rows — 24 findings and 6 STANDS. 11 WRONG, 1 BREAKS_LAW, 10 MISSING,
2 UNMEASURED_AS_FACT, 6 STANDS.**

Three are load-bearing and hold the slice: **F1** (a skipped chunk writes a different gap byte from
the cell pass, and no golden leg covers a skipped chunk), **F2** (the cavern lattice's tangential
edge nodes are clamped inside the chunk while the radial nodes are not, so a cave wall steps at every
chunk boundary) and **F3** (a tube cave dead-ends at a 512 m region wall). One more is close behind:
**F4**, the biome pole is `+Y` while the world's orbital reference plane is `z = 0`, and the golden
table has now frozen that.

Note: `crates/terrain/src/height.rs` and `src/body.rs` changed on disk while this review ran (an
octave-table compile-time assertion, two test tightenings). Every row below was re-checked against
the file as it stands now (`md5` recorded at read time); the three load-bearing rows are unaffected.
