**Summary.** 21 findings and 8 stands. WRONG 6 · BREAKS_LAW 0 · MISSING 12 · UNMEASURED_AS_FACT 3 · STANDS 8.

The plant's SKELETON is sound: every declared discriminant matches its enum's declaration order, no
field was appended to an existing wire struct, `ChildFelt` is the only new inter-shard arm and it is
represented in all five closed-set tests, and nothing from a NO row appears. The defects are (a) one
decode hole that a hostile or old peer can drive today, (b) one Tier-A coverage hole that will turn the
HR5 gate red, (c) two duplicated meanings the plant froze into the wire, and (d) a set of ★ notes and
ledger claims that describe code that does not exist.
