## What the refuter tried to refute and could NOT

1. **The lead comes from delivered data alone (SL10 clause 7).** It does. `lead_cursor` adds the
   buffer the wire's own contract states (`render_clock.rs:83`, `channels.rs:460`). Both ends of the
   offset are SAMPLES of delivered poses: `EntityTrack::sample` picks the two retained poses that
   bracket the cursor, and past the newest it takes the newest at both ends and freezes
   (`interp.rs:296-308`). No code divides a displacement by a time, and no code multiplies a speed by
   a lead. The lead moves the ladder's QUESTION only; the picture still draws at the drawn cursor
   (`terrain.rs:544`). The pilot inside a hull is the good example: the pilot stands still in the
   hull, and the lead comes from the planet's row moving through the hull's frame.
2. **Nothing new crosses a realm boundary (SL6).** The four new stamp fields ride `DevTerrainStamp`
   (`crates/devproto/src/state.rs:458-470`), which is the dev-control surface `vdctl` reads. No
   `InterShardFlow` arm changed, and no wire contract changed. `git diff --stat` names no file under
   `crates/wire`.
3. **The territory rule leaves NO ring of ground unclassified.** For a distance `d` the rule draws
   rung `r`, with `switch(r-1) < d <= switch(r)`. That column's geometry gives
   `near = d − 43.8·2^r` and `far = d + 43.8·2^r` — half the chunk's diagonal is
   `62 · 2^r · sqrt(2) / 2` (`ladder_view.rs:487-497`). `near < switch(r) = 869·2^r` holds, because
   `d <= 869·2^r`. `far > fade_in[1] = 0.55 · switch(r) = 478·2^r` holds, because
   `d > switch(r-1) = 434·2^r`. So the column the picture draws at `d` is always URGENT. A chunk the
   rule classes MARGIN is either covered whole by the finer rung, or it sits in the fade past its own
   switch where the coarser rung stands. The fade bands produce no false green. The false green is
   R4-1's, and it comes from the horizon's hysteresis.
4. **`culled` cannot grow without bound (SL9).** `wanted` builds a fresh local `culled` on each
   descent and assigns it at the end (`ladder_view.rs:718` and `:764`). Its size is bounded by the far
   columns ONE descent visits. `kept` is rebuilt the same way. The empty-eye path clears both
   (`:623-624`).
5. **`kept` and `culled` are disjoint by construction.** A culled column returns before `descend`
   (`:734-735`), so it emits no chunk key, and `kept` is built from the keys alone (`:763`). The
   test's `is_disjoint` assertion at `:1029` cannot fail, which is why it proves the construction and
   not the run.
6. **The gap is a lookup per urgent chunk, never a scan of the lane.** `urgent_missing` iterates the
   urgent set and calls the predicate (`ladder_view.rs:343`), and the predicate is
   `lane.is_resident(realm, key)`, a `BTreeSet::contains` (`chunks.rs:835`). Nothing walks the
   resident set. The cost still runs three times a frame — R4-20.
7. **`under_eye` names the right body for a pilot inside a hull over a planet.** `with_bodies` holds
   only the realms the lane gave a body to (`terrain.rs:565`), and the lane makes a body from a
   STATED surface (`:551`). A hull states no surface, so a hull is never a candidate, and the planet
   wins the `min_by` on height over the surface (`:765-771`).
8. **The hull's speed reading is sound for a coasting hull.** `measure_speed` reads the planet's row
   centre twice, 2 s apart, and divides by the elapsed wall time (`terrain_moving_eye.rs:414-423`).
   The pilot stands inside the hull, so the planet's row moves through the hull's frame at the hull's
   own speed. The planet's spin does not move its centre, and its orbit does not enter, because the
   hull is a child of the planet's realm. The walker's speed comes from its own row
   (`terrain_moving_eye.rs:767-782`), which is the walker's own delivered pose.
9. **The diagnosis "a throughput wall, not a lead shortfall" survives the numbers.** The probe's gap
   is 1 822 chunks over six rungs while the queue holds 2 798 and never falls under 900 (§16.2). A
   longer lead adds requests to a queue that already never drains, so a longer lead cannot close the
   gap. §16.3 says the build rate is UNMEASURED and owes it to M8-2's census, which is honest. The
   refuter found no reading that points at the lead. Two cautions stand: `DEFERRED` item 9 drops the
   UNMEASURED mark, and the probe starts with an inherited queue (R4-24).
10. **The skyline's hysteresis damps a flip on the skyline.** Once a far column clears and emits a
    chunk, the next descent judges it with `KEEP_MARGIN_RAD` 0.05, five times `WANT_MARGIN_RAD` 0.01
    (`ladder_view.rs:728-732`). The column must fall about 2.3° further under the wall before the
    descent culls it again. The hole is the ancestor that emits nothing — R4-9.
